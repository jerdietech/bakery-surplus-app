Storage guide

General storage reminders—not specific expiry guarantees

🍞 Plain breads

Keep in a clean, dry, airtight container at room temperature. Avoid direct sunlight and moisture.

🥛 Breads with dairy or cream fillings

Follow the bakery's handling instructions. Refrigerate when required and avoid leaving perishable fillings at room temperature for extended periods.

🧁 Filled or topped pastries

Storage needs depend on the ingredients. Check the product label or ask bakery staff for safe storage instructions.

💡 Reminder

Always check product freshness, ingredients, and bakery-provided storage instructions before consuming.
